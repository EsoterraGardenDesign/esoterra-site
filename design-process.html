Design process

How a garden takes shape

Our garden design process follows a clear structure, while allowing flexibility at each stage. Every project is shaped around you and your site, with elements of the process adapted to suit what the garden needs.

Get in touch

Our process

From first visit to first bloom.

01

Consultation and brief

We visit the garden, discuss what you would like to achieve and recommend the most suitable level of design support.

Garden survey

The site is carefully measured and recorded, giving us the accurate information needed to draw and design the space.

02
03

Moodboards and initial design

We prepare mood-boards to communicate the proposed style, materials and planting character. We then turn this direction into a concept plan for you to review.

Detailed plans

Once the overall garden design is agreed, we produce the practical information needed to bring it to life. This typically includes a master layout plan, along with construction details and material specifications. The planting design then follows, with a detailed planting plan, full plant list and schedule. 3D visuals can also be produced to help bring the ideas to life.

04
05

Construction and planting

You can use the plans with your own contractor or ask us to coordinate the build using landscapers we know and trust. We can then source the plants, lay out the scheme and complete the planting.
